Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sisyphus?

Sisyphus is a Latin vocabulary learning app designed to help you learn and retain words through spaced repetition.

How does the Leitner system work?

Sisyphus uses the Leitner system to organize words into different stacks based on how well you know them. Words you remember are reviewed less frequently, while words you struggle with are reviewed more often.

Why don't I have any words to review?

Sisyphus only shows words that are currently due for review. If you have completed your available reviews, there may simply be no words due at the moment.

What happens when I answer a word correctly?

Your answer affects the word's position in the Leitner system. Correct answers allow words to progress, while incorrect answers bring them back for more frequent practice.

Can I create and organize vocabulary lists?

Yes. Sisyphus lets you work with vocabulary lists so that you can keep different sets of Latin words organized for your studies.

Is my progress saved?

Your learning progress is associated with your account and synchronized with Sisyphus's backend so that your progress can be maintained between sessions.

I'm having trouble logging in. What should I do?

First, make sure you have an active internet connection and try signing in again. If the problem persists, contact support and include a description of what happens when you try to log in.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please contact support and describe what you were doing when the problem occurred, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Screenshots are helpful when applicable.

Account & Privacy

How is my account data handled?

Sisyphus uses account authentication and stores the information necessary to provide your account and learning experience. We aim to keep the information we store limited to what is needed to operate the app.

How can I request help with my account?

Contact support using the email address below and include the email address associated with your account. Do not send your password or authentication tokens.

We're happy to help with bugs, account problems, or other questions about Sisyphus.
